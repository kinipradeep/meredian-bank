A failing model is skipped for the cool-down period (15 minutes for a missing key or retired model), so later turns go straight to one that works. The X-ray panel shows which model answered.

The local model is the range's built-in, rule-based training model. It runs on this server, needs no key and never refuses a lab it's meant to fall for.
