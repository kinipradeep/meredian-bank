This notice explains what the Meridian Bank AI security range collects about you, why, who can see it, and how long it is kept. The range is run by {{ op }}, which decides how your data is used (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or "controller" elsewhere).

A training environment, monitored by your trainers. Meridian Bank is fictional: its customers, accounts, money and documents are made up. Trainers and administrators can read everything you type to the agents. Never enter real personal, financial or confidential information.

What we collect

  • Your account: {% if facts.google %}the name, email address and profile picture link from your Google account when you sign in with Google{% else %}your name and email address{% endif %}; your role; when you joined and last signed in; and when you accepted these policies. Administrators with a local password: a salted scrypt hash of it, never the password itself.
  • What you do in the labs: your messages to the agents and their replies, including the tools they called, the documents they retrieved and the security checks that ran ("traces"); labs you completed, steps and answers you opened, and flags you submitted.
  • Records the agents create while you attack them, such as refunds, transfers, loan offers, FAQ answers and simulated emails. Nothing is ever sent outside the range.
  • An activity log: sign-ins, policy acceptance, admin actions, and for each agent turn the model used, token counts, timing and guard results.
  • A fictional bank profile: when you first sign in we generate a made-up customer ID, accounts and transactions for you. They are test data, not information about you.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used in memory to slow down repeated wrong passwords and is not stored in the range's database. The server's system logs may record IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and troubleshooting, and rotate them automatically.

Why we use it

  • To run the labs: the agents need your messages to answer, and the range needs your progress to show what you have completed.
  • So trainers can teach: they review conversations, progress and which answers were opened, to help you and to grade sessions they run.
  • To keep the range secure and fair: to detect abuse, enforce the acceptable use policy and investigate problems.
  • To improve the labs: for example by seeing which labs most people get stuck on.

We rely on the consent you give when you accept these policies. You can withdraw it at any time (see Your rights); the range cannot be used without it. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking tools.

How we protect it

Encrypted HTTPS connections; access to the admin area only for named administrators, with lockout after repeated wrong passwords; provider API keys kept where the agents cannot reach them; secrets encrypted in the database; a firewall that exposes only the website; and automatic security updates on the server. The range deliberately contains vulnerable AI agents, but they work on fictional data; please don't give them real data.

Cookies

The range sets one cookie, airange_session. It is essential: it keeps you signed in and protects forms against cross-site attacks. It expires 12 hours after you sign in, or when you sign out. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.
