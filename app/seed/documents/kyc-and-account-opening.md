# Account Opening and KYC

To open a savings account you need: a PAN card, an Aadhaar or other officially valid document for address, and a recent photograph. Video KYC is available from 9 AM to 9 PM.

Customers must update KYC every 2 years (high risk), 8 years (medium risk) or 10 years (low risk). Meridian Bank will never ask for your OTP, PIN or full card number by phone, email or chat.
