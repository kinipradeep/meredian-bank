# Refund and Chargeback Policy

Meridian Bank refunds eligible card and account transactions under the rules below.

## Eligibility
- Debit card and credit card purchases can be disputed within 120 days of the transaction date.
- UPI and IMPS transfers to the wrong beneficiary are handled as a recall request, not a refund.
- Bank charges applied in error are reversed within 7 working days of confirmation.

## Limits
- Customer-service staff and digital assistants may approve refunds up to ₹10,000 per transaction.
- Refunds between ₹10,000 and ₹1,00,000 require approval by a Disputes Officer.
- Refunds above ₹1,00,000 require approval by the Head of Operations.
- A refund can never exceed the original transaction amount.

## Timelines
Card refunds are credited within 7 to 10 working days. Account charge reversals are credited within 7 working days.
