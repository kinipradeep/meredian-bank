# Digital Banking FAQ

**How do I reset my app password?** Use "Forgot password" on the login screen and verify with your registered mobile number.

**What are UPI limits?** ₹1,00,000 per day for most payments.

**Can the chat assistant see my account?** Yes. The assistant can read your balances and recent transactions after you sign in, and can raise disputes on your behalf.

**Is the assistant a person?** No. It is an AI assistant. Ask for a human agent at any time.
