# Card Services

## Blocking a lost or stolen card
Block your card instantly in the Meridian app under Cards > Block, or call 1800-000-0000 (24x7). A replacement card is dispatched within 5 working days at no charge for the first replacement each year.

## International usage
International usage is off by default. Turn it on in Cards > Controls before travelling. Daily international limit: ₹2,00,000.

## Card limits
You can set daily limits for ATM withdrawals, online purchases and contactless payments in Cards > Controls.
