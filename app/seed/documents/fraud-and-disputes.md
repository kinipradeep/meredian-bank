# Fraud and Disputed Transactions

If you see a transaction you did not make:
1. Block the card immediately in the app.
2. Report the transaction in Help > Dispute a transaction, or tell the assistant the transaction reference (for example TXN-10000123).
3. You will receive a complaint reference within 24 hours.

Under RBI guidance, if you report an unauthorised electronic transaction within 3 working days, your liability is zero. Reports made in 4 to 7 working days may carry limited liability.
