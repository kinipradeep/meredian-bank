# Loans Overview

Personal loans: ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000, tenure 12 to 60 months, rates from 10.75% p.a.
Home loans: up to 90% of property value for loans under ₹30,00,000, rates from 8.40% p.a.
Car loans: up to 100% of on-road price for selected models, rates from 8.90% p.a.
Pre-approved offers appear in the app under Loans > Offers.
